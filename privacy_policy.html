2. How we use information

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Services and fulfill your tickets and orders.
  • Communicate confirmations, proofs, invoices, pickup/shipping, and support.
  • Prevent fraud and reward abuse, secure the Services, and comply with law.
  • Aggregate, de-identified analytics (e.g., occupancy counts). We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your private data to train AI models.

3. When we share

  • Organizers — for an event you buy into, the Organizer receives the information needed to admit and serve you (e.g., that you hold a valid ticket).
  • Print Partners — for a merch or shipped-reward order, the assigned Print Partner receives your name, shipping address, and artwork solely to fulfill it.
  • Payment processor (Stripe) — to take payment and pay out sellers.
  • Service providers under contract (hosting, email/SMS), legal requirements, and business transfers.
  • We do not sell or rent your contact lists.

4. Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the Services, meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements, then delete or de-identify it. Occupancy counts are stored in aggregate.

5. Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, to opt out of "sale/share" (we do not sell), and to be free from discrimination for exercising rights. To make a request, contact privacy@deaddance.app; we will verify and respond as required by law. You can also decline location features and clear device storage at any time.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No system is perfectly secure; please use the Services responsibly and keep your contact details current.

7. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International users

We operate in the United States; if you use the Services from elsewhere, you consent to processing in the U.S. [If serving the EU/UK, add lawful-transfer mechanism and any required representative.]
