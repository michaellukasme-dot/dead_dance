Festival Flow & Crowd Analytics

Aggregate, consented, de-identified crowd movement — the institutional data an organizer buys. Stage attendance, dwell, corridor flow, stage→vendor attribution, and zone heat, every cohort suppressed below 20 people.
⚠️ DEMO DATA — synthetic. These are illustrative numbers for the pitch, NOT real festival data. They are replaced by live data once native GPS is validated on device. Nothing here was measured from a real phone yet.
How this stays legal & private: data comes from an opt-in, background-GPS sample under a rotating, de-identified token — never an identity. Every number is an aggregate cohort count; any cohort under 20 people is dropped, never shown. GNSS is 3–10 m, so everything resolves to a zone/geofence (e.g. "the Beer Garden"), never a specific unit. There is no individual-journey view — that is out of scope by design. 🌹
