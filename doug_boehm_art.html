Doug Boehm

Painter and illustrator. Doug's personal work has hung in galleries across the United States and lives in private collections in the US, UK, Australia, and Taiwan. His illustration has run in national publications and on tour posters for the bands you follow.

He works mostly in acrylic — on canvas, wood panel, records, books, skateboards, vintage envelopes — building dense, luminous worlds of kings and serpents, horsemen and green skulls, all rays and roots and nerve.

Represented by Arch Enemy Arts, Philadelphia. Based in Pennsylvania, with his wife and three children.

Hi Doug — here's the idea

This is not unlike your Big Cartel — your posters, your prints, your painted Strats, your prices. The difference is where it stands. Instead of waiting for people to find the shop, your store sits inside the DeadDance map in front of the ~1.7 million people who come through MusikFest — and behind the Doug Boehm button every one of them taps to see your poster. Same shop you already run; a crowd you don't have to go find.

🎫 A second counter — sell posters to the bands, all year.

MusikFest is just the front door. You already make gig posters — Puscifer, Primus, the room-shakers — show after show, tour after tour. On DeadDance there are hundreds of acts who need art for every date they play. Offer yours, and your poster rides their event page, their ticket, and their merch — not for one festival, but ongoing, every show they book. Fans buy the print; the band commissions the poster. One studio, two kinds of buyer, working year-round.

If you like it, the rest is your skin in the game — you send the catalogue and share your link, and we stand it up in front of the crowd.
