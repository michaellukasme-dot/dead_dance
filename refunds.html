Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026 · Version 1.0

⚖️ Draft for legal review. Confirm the defaults below (ticket refundability, service-fee treatment, tax) with counsel and with each Organizer's own rules before launch. Consumer-protection laws in some regions grant rights that override "all sales final."

1. Tickets

  • Sold by the Organizer. The Organizer sets whether a ticket is refundable or exchangeable. Unless the event listing or the Organizer states otherwise, ticket sales are final.
  • Event canceled. If an Organizer cancels an event, you are entitled to a refund of the ticket's face value. The platform service fee is [refundable / non-refundable — confirm]. Refunds are issued to the original payment method through our processor.
  • Event postponed or materially changed. Your ticket is typically honored for the new date; if you cannot attend a rescheduled event, refund eligibility follows the Organizer's rules and applicable law.
  • Free RSVPs carry no charge and simply reserve entry; you can release your RSVP at any time so the spot returns to the pool.
  • How to request. Contact support@deaddance.app with your order details. Chargebacks filed without contacting us first may delay resolution.

2. Band & venue merchandise (custom printed)

  • Made to order. Shirts are printed to your fabric, size mix, and artwork. Because they are personalized, they are not returnable or refundable once approved for printing, except as below.
  • You approve first. We send a proof and an invoice before printing. Nothing prints, and you are not charged, until you approve — please review your proof carefully, as approval authorizes production.
  • Defects & our errors. If your order arrives defective, damaged, or materially different from the approved proof, contact us within [e.g., 14 days] of receipt with photos and we will reprint or refund the affected items.
  • Cancellation. You may cancel before you approve the proof at no cost. After approval, an order that has entered production cannot be canceled.

3. Street-team rewards

Cookies and free shirts have no cash value and are not refundable, redeemable for cash, or transferable. A claimed free shirt is picked up in person during the eligible festival window; unclaimed rewards may expire.

4. Taxes & fees

Where refunds are due, applicable sales tax is refunded with the item price. Platform service fees are handled as stated above. [Confirm your sales-tax registration and collection obligations for tickets and goods in each state where you operate.]

5. How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method via our processor (Stripe) and may take several business days to appear, depending on your bank.

6. Contact

support@deaddance.app · We'll confirm receipt and walk you through it.
