1The Job

Four moves at every act. That's it. Illustrative — the real thing is even faster.

Hand the band their ticket and 3 shirts — theirs to keep or throw to the crowd.

Pass the free ticket to fans and show them how to touch phones to receive it.

Each fan passes it to the next. The turn-and-share is the whole point — you start the chain.
