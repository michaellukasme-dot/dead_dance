The Data Report — what MOST buys

Attendance and crowd intelligence you can't get any other way — all aggregate, privacy-clean, defensible. Built on the crowd the map already moves. We can show a representative sample report for both Musikfest and the Great Allentown Fair.

  • Unique visitors per day — deduped per phone (the local who runs home for lunch counts once).
  • Live occupancy per Platz — how many people are in each area right now (ops + public safety).
  • Footfall that scales with the stage — a huge crowd around a small stage reads as off-the-charts density.
  • Dwell, arrivals-by-hour, new-vs-returning, busiest Platz, peak hour.

Why it's the product: no names, no tracking, no facial recognition, no data sold — k-anonymous aggregates only, short retention. That posture is why a festival and its safety partners can accept it. And the per-Platz daily footfall is the hard proof that lets you price and renew every naming sponsor with numbers instead of estimates — a book that dwarfs the data fee.
